# Momentum, top / bottom 30% (allfirm breakpoints)

Winners = top 30% of stocks on past return (t-12 to t-2), Losers = bottom 30%, WML = Winners − Losers. Everything else as in results/tables_<scheme>.md.

Numbers in [brackets] are t-stats. "x100" coefficients are in % per month.
IB = 1 if the market fell over the past 24 months (bear market). IU = 1 if the market is up this month.
Rm = market excess return. var_m = variance of daily market returns over the past 126 days (annualized).
